QTimer is used to perform periodic operations 
without blocking the GUI. 
After the device is connected, 
the timer can be started with an interval 
of 1000 milliseconds (1 second). 
Its timeout signal is connected to a 
function such as requestReading(). 
Every second, this function requests 
or generates a simulated device reading 
and sends it to the GUI for display. 
When disconnected, the timer is stopped.

example: 

timer = QTimer()
timer.timeout.connect(requestReading)
timer.start(1000)
